This page is for smaller thoughts I have that I would like to share.

The cause of hatred is a question I have been thinking about for years and still don't have an answer too. But, I think I have gotten close with some aspects. I think much of the time we hate specific aspects about people, its because those are aspects of ourselves we either hate, or are trying to get rid of. And this external person is holding up a mirror to our own self image, and asking us "Are you like me?". I would guess this happens because of peoples natural tendency to compare themselves to the people around them.

Lately I have been wondering if it is possible to cause split personality disorder, on purpose. Like a science fiction idea of purposely causing it, then making some kind of intentional mental illusion to represent this extra personality. Like a ghost.

I started doing this thing where I give my thoughts like a grounding visual space in my head. So that while thinking I can represent the thoughts and emotions as physical things. I currently am keeping it as an ocean, where I walk on the water of it, and whales swim below. I chose this because I can make anything out of the water. I find doing this really helps me think and not get side tracked.
