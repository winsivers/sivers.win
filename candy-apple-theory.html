Candy Apple Theory

I think that everyone is like a candy apple: their real personality - the apple - is covered by a coating that may be sugary-sweet, or pungently tart, or whatever flavour it may have. Some are so heavy you can't even glimpse the apple. Some are fuzzy, so you can only recognise a blurry outline of the apple. Some coverings are practically transparent, revealing almost all that's inside, although there is still a thin film that clings. Meeting a person who looks like a plain, uncoated apple is quite rare.
