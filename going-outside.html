Going outside.

I think a lot of people my age stay inside too much. I like the indoors too, but the only opportunities indoors are online, whereas from my experience, you need a combination, a balance, of both indoors and outdoors to get the most out of life. Going outdoors to meet people and find places, and staying indoors to apply to things and relax. I am not saying "go out and touch some grass." Nature is nice, but going out into the city and just hanging out, even if you're alone, I think can give you more ideas and things to do. Whereas, if you stay indoors, you can do plenty of productive things, and in a more comfortable place; however, it cuts you off from meeting new people in your community and is less healthy.
