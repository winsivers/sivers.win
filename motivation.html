Religion

The concept of life after death is a very comforting belief that I think is useful. Therefore, I spent a very long time attempting to find a religion that, from the perspective of an atheist, was believable. However, despite combing through Christianity, Buddhism, Hinduism, Islam, and multiple others, I still didn't find solace in any of them for different reasons. I thought Christianity sounded too man-made, it fulfilled my every wish too much, having someone watching over you, having a sense of justice, and many other things, to the point it made it less credible. Buddhism, while a great religion, had one major flaw in my opinion: the urge to detach from the world. I think that while that is the easiest way to specifically avoid suffering, my goals in life are not just to avoid suffering. And so on and so on with different reasons. Until I decided if I couldn't find one, I would think of a useful belief. Because of a dental procedure, I was unable to speak for about a week. So I spent this week spending roughly 70 hours re-learning how to speak. But, in the silent moments, I was questioning what happens after death, and after a few days, it came to me. The perfect belief that aligns with my values. qualia, I thought of the idea of a sentient soul becoming quale after we die. Like the warm glow of a sparkler at sunset or mist rolling over a mountain. I found this useful because it reframed death as a beautiful transformation rather than an ending. While also not being so fulfilling it's unbelievable. And it aligns with my values and beliefs.

As a side note, I think it is okay for people to believe whatever they want.
