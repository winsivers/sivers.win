Candy Apple Theroy

I think that everyone is like a candy apple: their real personality - the apple - is covered by a coating that may be sugary-sweet, or pungently tart, or whatever flavour it may have. Some are so heavy you can't even glimpse the apple. Some are fuzzy, so you can only recognise a blurry outline of the apple. Some coverings are practically transparent, revealing almost all that's inside, although there is still a thin film that clings. Meeting a person who looks like a plain, uncoated apple is quite rare.

Going outdoors to meet people and find places, and staying indoors to apply to things and relax. Motivation

I see motivation as a under-rated skill. And discipline as a over-rated skill. At least from what I have seen my friends do, and things I have done. Motivation doesn't just kick you off half way then stop, if you do it right it takes you all the way. Where as discipline is helpful, and gives you better results, its less necessary than it seems. To me at least discipline is forcing yourself to do something even when you don t want too, but if your motivated to do that thing, just by making your own motivation, you can do the same thing as if you were exercising discipline, but now with more joy.

Kindness

This is my favorite social strategy to make friends. That I think more teenagers need to know, trying your best to just be nice to everyone, absolutely everyone, even if you hate their guts and wish they would break a bone, still just be nice, and do what you know is the tequniquely "right" thing to do. Even if you don't wnat to, so, lets say someone uses your waterbottle to pour water onto your seat where you were just sitting, while I was tempted to pour the remaining water in the bottle onto their head, exercise restraint, and just walk away. And next time you see them, genuinely smile at them. It makes you more likeable, and gives you the knowledge that you when going to sleep at night can know that today, you didn't cause anyone harm.
