Kindness

This is my favorite social strategy to make friends. That I think more teenagers need to know, trying your best to just be nice to everyone, absolutely everyone, even if you hate their guts and wish they would break a bone, still just be nice, and do what you know is the tequniquely "right" thing to do. Even if you don't want to, so, lets say someone uses your waterbottle to pour water onto your seat where you were just sitting, while I was tempted to pour the remaining water in the bottle onto their head, exercise restraint, and just walk away. And next time you see them, genuinely smile at them. It makes you more likeable, and gives you the knowledge that you when going to sleep at night can know that today, you didn't cause anyone harm.
