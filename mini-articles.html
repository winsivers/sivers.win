Mini-Articles

These are a collection of smaller thoughts or ideas that I would like to share.

The cause of hatred is a question I have been thinking about for years and still don't have an answer to. I think I have gotten close with some aspects. I think much of the time we hate specific aspects about people, it's because those are aspects of ourselves we either hate or are trying to get rid of. And this external person is holding up a mirror to our own self-image, and asking us "Are you like me?". I would guess this happens because of people's natural tendency to compare themselves to the people around them.

Lately I have started envisioning my thoughts, beliefs and feelings in a physical space to help link me to the ideas and keep me focused by grounding me in one location. This location right now is an ocean that I am balanced on top of.

I had this idea that I really like of how to make life look more beautiful. Looking for the reflections about light or where light passes through an object. After I started doing this. I have noticed I have become much happier.
